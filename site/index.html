How this works

Data

  • Live output: AEMO 5-minute SCADA for each battery (Dispatch SCADA). Positive is discharging into the grid, negative is charging.
  • Actual stored energy: AEMO publishes each battery's telemetered energy (MWh) in the Next Day Dispatch file, the morning after. That's the solid line.
  • Batteries and sizes: AEMO registration data (MW, MWh, charge and discharge efficiency). Only bidirectional units in SA of 5 MW or more.
  • Prices: SA 5-minute dispatch price, then AEMO's 5-minute pre-dispatch (next hour) and 30-minute pre-dispatch forecasts.

Estimate

  • Start from the last actual stored energy AEMO has published (end of the last trading day, 4 am NEM time).
  • Step forward every 5 minutes: discharging takes MW × 5 min ÷ discharge efficiency out; charging puts MW × 5 min × charge efficiency in. Capped between empty and full.
  • Efficiencies start from AEMO's registered values. If a single round-trip factor fits the last 14 days clearly better, the site uses that instead.
  • Each morning, the new actuals replace the estimate and the clock restarts. So the estimate never runs more than about 30 hours from real data.

Caveats

  • Model fit is the average gap between estimate and actual over the last 14 days, as a share of capacity. Good is under 5%, fair is 5 to 10%, low is over 10%.
  • SCADA is a snapshot each 5 minutes, so fast FCAS and regulation movements in between are missed.
  • Some sites report stored energy unevenly, which shows up as a low model fit.
  • Usable capacity is the larger of the registered MWh and the most energy AEMO has ever reported for that battery.
  • Updates run on GitHub's scheduler and can land a few minutes late.
Data: AEMO NEMweb (public). Estimates only, not investment advice.
